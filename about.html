About the studio

Our passion is music.

Joanna Self, Director of Making Melodies Music in Castle Rock, CO.

I’m Joanna. I direct Making Melodies Music here in Castle Rock. Everyone has a song. We help you develop the skills you need to bring that song to life.

A qualified instructor will bring quality instruction to your musical abilities. I teach as a qualified vocal coach with two master’s degrees, and I work with students from beginner to advanced piano as well.

In voice we work on basic breathing techniques, how to expand your knowledge of singing, broadening your range, and having a great time doing so. In piano we learn how to sight read music, play by ear, and even create your piano pieces — with basic theory taught and applied in every lesson.

Begin piano lessons, voice lessons, or guitar lessons with an instructor today in Castle Rock, CO. Write me and we will find the plan that fits.
