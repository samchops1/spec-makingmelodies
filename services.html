Lessons we offer

Quality instruction for your musical abilities.

Begin piano lessons, voice lessons, or guitar lessons with an instructor today in Castle Rock, CO!

Beginner to Advanced Piano

From beginners to advanced piano, learn how to sight read music, play by ear (hearing a song and playing it), and even create your piano pieces! Basic theory will be taught and applied in every lesson and will prepare you to even take on another instrument in the future!

Beginner to Advanced Vocal

Take the opportunity to be trained by a qualified vocal coach with two master’s degrees. Learn basic breathing techniques, how to expand your knowledge of singing, broadening your range, and having a great time doing so!

Guitar lessons

Guitar is offered in Half Hour and 45-Minute lesson plans, with piano and voice. One Hour plans list piano and voice.
